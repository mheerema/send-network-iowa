Joel Rister of GuideStone wrote for the Baptist Convention of Iowa on three things ministers here tend to miss. Ministers in the SBC Church Retirement Plan can have retirement income designated as housing allowance, which carries the tax benefit into retirement. "Housing allowance doesn't stop when you retire." Ministers whose church contributes at least $50 a month may qualify for benefits provided free through the BCI: a survivor benefit of up to $100,000 and disability income of up to $500 a month for as long as five years. And GuideStone keeps minister-specific tools on taxes, housing allowance, and compensation.

Fifty dollars a month is within reach of nearly every church in Iowa, and it unlocks protection most bivocational pastors are living without. If you pastor here, or you handle the finances of a church that has a pastor, check whether that contribution is in place.
