**What it is.** Joel Rister of GuideStone, writing for the Baptist Convention of Iowa, lays out three benefits ministers here tend to miss:

1. Retirement income from the SBC Church Retirement Plan can be designated as housing allowance. "Housing allowance doesn't stop when you retire," Rister writes; the tax benefit carries into retirement.
2. A church contribution of $50 or more a month may qualify a minister for free benefits provided through the BCI: a survivor benefit of up to $100,000 and disability income of up to $500 a month for up to five years.
3. GuideStone publishes minister-specific tools on taxes, housing allowance, and compensation.

**Who it's for.** Every pastor in Iowa, and whoever handles the church's books. Bivocational pastors stand to gain the most; many are living without this protection.

**One step.** Check whether your church's $50-a-month retirement contribution is in place. If it isn't, that is the first item for your next budget conversation.
