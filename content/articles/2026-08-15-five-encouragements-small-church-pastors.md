If you preach on Sunday and clock in somewhere else Monday morning, this episode is aimed at you.

Mark Clifton and Mark Hallock gave an episode of the Revitalize & Replant podcast to five encouragements for pastors of small churches. Underneath all five sits one claim they make explicitly: meaningful ministry is never measured by attendance.

The encouragements are pastoral and specific. Jesus sees every act of faithfulness, including the ones nobody counts and nobody thanks you for. The people in your congregation are the ministry itself, and loving them well is the assignment you were given. You serve under a higher Shepherd, which means the flock was His before it was yours and the results are His to produce.

Put it on in the truck this week. Then send it to one other pastor in your association who has gone quiet lately.

It belongs next to the numbers we posted Thursday. Those told us the 70-person church is the normal church. This one tells us what to do on the inside of it.
