NAMB has profiled Carl Wimmer, pastor of First Baptist Church in Duchesne, Utah, a town of 1,300 people. The church has held standing-room services since 2021 and has baptized former Mormons and oil workers. "We're a town of 1,300 people, and we have 100 people who attend church. Think about that. And I'm telling you, with God, all things are possible."

This year has been the hardest of Wimmer's life. He lost his 24-year-old son Julian in January. Nine days later another son was struck by a vehicle. Then Carl himself suffered cardiac arrest. NAMB and Utah-Idaho Baptists covered the funeral and medical costs, replaced a vehicle, and are helping fund a $50,000 expansion of the church's building.

A town of 1,300 with a growing church is the Iowa county-seat scenario. What God is doing in Duchesne is what we pray for in dozens of towns here. And when Wimmer's world caved in, cooperative Southern Baptist giving showed up as real money and real help. That is the family of churches we belong to.
