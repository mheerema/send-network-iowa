Walker Armstrong has a new book from the North American Mission Board, *The Way of Fruitfulness: Becoming a More Productive Leader*. Send Network is running a free hour around it on Thursday, October 1 at 3:30 Eastern, with Brandon Moore and JimBo Stewart joining him. If you followed the Replant Bootcamp podcast, Stewart is the reason to look twice.

> You weren't called to be busy. You were called to be fruitful.
>
> — via Send Network

The book follows a stressed-out pastor named Joel Birch through seven shifts: intentionality, interruptions, hearing God's direction, setting real goals, following through. The event page is careful to say none of it is borrowed from business productivity writing, which is the kind of claim a publisher makes about a book it published. October will settle that.

Who this is for in Iowa: the bivocational replanter whose week is gone by Wednesday and whose actual work never starts. In a church you pastor on top of a job, the calendar is the constraint.

Register now and put October 1, 3:30 Eastern on your calendar. Six weeks is enough lead time to defend one hour. If you cannot defend one hour six weeks out, you already know what the webinar is going to tell you.
