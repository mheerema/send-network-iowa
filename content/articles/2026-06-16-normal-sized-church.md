At the SBC Annual Meeting in Orlando this June, NAMB hosted a luncheon for small church pastors. Mark Clifton, who leads NAMB's replanting work, put a number on it: 70% of Southern Baptist churches run fewer than 100 people, and most are led by bivocational pastors. "These normal-sized churches are in every community, every crossroad, every major metropolitan area of our nation, and most of them are pastored by bivocational pastors."

Clifton, Andy Addis, and Mark Hallock urged pastors to root their identity in Christ rather than in attendance, and the convention passed a resolution honoring bivocational and volunteer pastors.

Normal-sized is the right word. A church of 60 or 80 is the normal Southern Baptist church, and nearly every congregation in the Baptist Convention of Iowa sits in that 70%. Clifton told the room to love the church they have, since only eternity will reveal the results of the labor. If you pastor one of Iowa's normal-sized churches, that word is for you.
